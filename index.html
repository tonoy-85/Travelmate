Popular Destinations

Cox's Bazar

Visit the world’s longest natural sea beach and experience the golden sands, roaring waves, and vibrant local culture. Explore scenic viewpoints, enjoy fresh seafood, and wander through the charming streets of this coastal paradise.

Bandarban

Discover the serene hills and lush landscapes of Bandarban, where misty mountains, cascading waterfalls, and vibrant tribal culture await. Trek through scenic trails and immerse yourself in nature’s untouched beauty.

Sylhet

Explore the enchanting and picturesque land of Sylhet, home to sprawling, lush tea gardens, sparkling waterfalls, serene rivers, and sacred shrines. Experience the rich local culture, vibrant traditions, and the refreshing natural beauty of northeastern Bangladesh.

Featured Activities

Sea Turtle Watching

Experience the magical **sea turtle watching** along Cox’s Bazar’s beaches during nesting season. Witness these gentle creatures lay eggs or hatchlings make their first journey to the sea, a truly rare and unforgettable sight.

Tribal Village Homestay

Experience a **Tribal Village Homestay** in Bandarban, living alongside local communities and learning their traditions. Participate in daily activities, enjoy authentic tribal cuisine, and explore handmade crafts for a truly unique cultural adventure.

Jaflong Stone Collection

At Jaflong, enjoy a unique **stone collection and river walk** along the sparkling Dawki River near the Meghalaya border. Witness locals collecting giant river stones, explore the scenic riverbanks, and soak in the stunning natural beauty of the area.
